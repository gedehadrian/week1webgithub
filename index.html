About the artist

Soft, colorful art — made with care.

I'm Hadrian, a digital artist based in Tangerang, Indonesia. I've been drawing since childhood and taking commissions professionally for years — from cozy chibi doodles to detailed character illustrations and cinematic scenes.

My style leans warm and expressive with clean linework, playful colors, and soft rendering. I love drawing OCs, VTuber references, couples, family portraits, pets, and brand mascots that feel alive.

Every commission is 100% hand-drawn — no AI generation. You'll get progress updates, sketch approval, and full commercial-safe files for personal use.

FAQ

Quick answers.

Do you take commercial commissions?

Yes! Add the commercial license (+50% of base price) and you can use the artwork for merchandise, marketing, book covers, streaming assets, etc.

What can I request?

OCs, VTuber refs, couples, family portraits, pets, fanart of your favorite characters, brand mascots, and stylized illustrations. NSFW is not accepted.

How do payments work?

50% deposit locks your slot. The remaining 50% is paid after you approve the final render and before you receive the high-res files.

Do you offer refunds?

Before sketch approval — full refund. After sketch approval — partial refund based on work completed. After final delivery — no refund.

Can I request revisions?

Yes, each package includes free revisions during the sketch and render stages. Additional major revisions after approval are billed per hour.
